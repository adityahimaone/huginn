01Foundation

Three rules before any component.

The system is a small set of decisions repeated with discipline. If a page needs a fourth rule to explain itself, the first three are not clear enough.

01 / Surface

One ground, one accent.

Most of the page is neutral. The accent marks action, not decoration.

02 / Hierarchy

One focal point.

Size, weight, contrast and space agree on what matters. Everything else steps back.

03 / Restraint

One signature.

One material, one motion moment, one loud field. Extra effects dilute the system.

02Material

Choose a material, not a filter.

This specimen uses glass as its one signature. Dither is shown as an alternative, not layered on top. Pick one; document the fallback.

Signature

Glass

Translucency, blur and an edge highlight for floating chrome. Keep copy on a solid plate; provide a solid fallback.

Alternative

Dither

A two-colour texture for images and atmosphere. Use Bayer for motion, error diffusion for stills; never under body copy.

07Motion

Motion has a job.

Feedback is fast; entrances are short; exits are shorter. If the interaction happens hundreds of times a day, it does not animate.

RoleDurationEasingUse
Press150msease-outbutton feedback
Popover160mscubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)menus, tooltips
Dialog240msease-outmodal, sheet

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, keep the finished state and remove movement. No state may be carried by motion alone.
